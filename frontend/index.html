Bright Futures Scholarship Tiers

The Florida Bright Futures Scholarship Program rewards Florida high school graduates for high academic achievement. Here are the core SAT requirements for the two main tiers.

Florida Academic Scholars (FAS)

100% Tuition & Applicable Fees
FAS

The highest tier award covering 100% of your tuition at Florida public institutions.

REQUIRED SAT SCORES
2024-2025 Grads: 1340
2025-2026 & Beyond: 1330

Florida Medallion Scholars (FMS)

75% Tuition & Applicable Fees
FMS

The second tier award covering 75% of your tuition at Florida public institutions.

REQUIRED SAT SCORES
2024-2025 Grads: 1210
2025-2026 & Beyond: 1190

Deadlines & Superscoring

Superscoring Policy

Bright Futures accepts SAT superscores. The program combines the highest Evidence-Based Reading & Writing score and the highest Math score across multiple test administrations to calculate the maximum composite score. SAT scores cannot be combined with ACT or CLT scores.

Additional Requirements

Along with your SAT scores, you must meet the following criteria:

Weighted GPA

FAS 3.5 Minimum
FMS 3.0 Minimum

Service or Work Hours

FAS 100 Volunteer Hours
or 100 Paid Work Hours
FMS 75 Volunteer Hours
or 100 Paid Work Hours
