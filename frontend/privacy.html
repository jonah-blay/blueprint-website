Effective Date: August 18, 2026

1. Information We Collect

At B&M Blueprint L.L.C., we collect information that you voluntarily provide to us when you fill out our contact inquiries or submit a review/testimonial. This includes your name, email address, student grade/score details, and the content of your message.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect solely for the purpose of communicating with you regarding your inquiry or displaying your testimonial on our platform.

3. Data Storage and Retention

  • Reviews & Testimonials: Review data will be stored securely for no longer than one calendar year. After this period, it is subject to deletion or anonymization.
  • Contact Form Inquiries: Emails and inquiries submitted through our contact form will be stored indefinitely to maintain a record of our communications with you.

4. Your Rights and Deletion Requests

You have the right to request the immediate takedown or deletion of your data at any time.

  • If you submitted a review/testimonial and would like it removed, please email us at bandmblueprint@gmail.com. We will process your takedown request immediately.
  • If you submitted a contact inquiry and wish for your email record to be permanently deleted from our system, please email us at bandmblueprint@gmail.com requesting deletion.

5. Third-Party Services

Our website utilizes Google reCAPTCHA v2 to prevent spam and abuse. Use of this service is subject to the Google Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.
